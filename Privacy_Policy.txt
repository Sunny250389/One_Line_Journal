Privacy Policy for One Line Journal
Effective Date: May 3, 2026

One Line Journal ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1) Information We Collect
- Journal content you type in the App.
- Basic app preferences/settings needed for functionality.

2) How Information Is Used
- Your journal data is used only to provide core journaling features.
- Data is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export/share it.
- We do not sell your personal information.

3) Data Sharing
- The App does not share your journal content with third parties by default.
- If you use Android system features (such as backups or sharing), data handling by those services is governed by their own privacy terms.

4) Data Retention
- Your entries remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
- Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored data, subject to device behavior.

5) Security
- We take reasonable steps in-app to protect your data. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6) Children's Privacy
- The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7) Your Choices
- You may edit or delete entries within the App.
- You may uninstall the App at any time to stop further data processing by the App.

8) Third-Party Services
- If the App integrates third-party services in future versions (analytics, crash reporting, cloud sync, ads), this policy will be updated accordingly.

9) Changes to This Policy
- We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new Effective Date.

10) Contact
For privacy questions, please contact the app publisher/developer through the app store listing or repository contact details.
