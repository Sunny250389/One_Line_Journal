Privacy Policy for One Line Journal
Effective Date: October 9, 2026

One Line Journal ("the App") is a personal journaling app. Your journal is yours: it is stored on your device, and the developer has no servers that receive it and no way to read it. This policy explains exactly what the App stores, when anything leaves your device, and how you can remove it.

1) Information the App Handles
On your device:
- Journal entries: the text you write (up to 120 characters per day), its date, and whether you marked it as a favorite.
- Your name, if you enter one when you log in. It is used only to personalize the greeting and the journal title.
- App settings: theme, font, text size, reminder time, and whether the app lock is on.
- If you log in with Google: the email address of that Google account, so the App knows which account is signed in.

The App does NOT collect: your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising ID, or device identifiers. It has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting services.

2) Google Login and Backup (optional)
Logging in with Google is optional. If you do, the App saves a backup copy of your journal entries to the hidden "App Data" folder of YOUR Google Drive account, using the permission "drive.appdata". This permission lets the App read and write only its own backup file; it cannot see your other Drive files. The backup is created automatically after you write, on app start, and on sign-in, and a failed backup is retried in the background when a connection is available.

This backup goes directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to the developer. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's own privacy policy and your Google account settings. If you sign in on another device with the same account, your journal is restored there. If you sign in with a different Google account, the journal on the device is replaced by that account's own backup, so one account's entries are never uploaded into another account.

If you use "Login with name" instead, nothing is sent anywhere and your journal stays on the device.

3) Android System Backup
Android may include the App's local data in its own device backup and device-to-device transfer (for example, Google's Android Backup). This is controlled by your device settings, handled by Google or your device maker, and governed by their policies.

4) Sharing
The App shares nothing unless you choose to. If you tap Share on an entry, the App creates an image of that entry and hands it to the app you pick. The image is kept temporarily in the App's private cache and is removed the next time you share or open the App.

5) App Lock and Biometrics
You can turn on an app lock that asks for your fingerprint, face, or device PIN, pattern or password. This uses Android's built-in system prompt. The App never receives, sees, or stores your fingerprint, face data, or PIN; Android only tells the App whether the check succeeded. While the app lock is on, the App blocks screenshots and hides its content in the recent-apps view.

6) Notifications
If you set a daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. No personal information is included in it and nothing is sent to a server.

7) Permissions
- Internet: only used for the optional Google Drive backup.
- Notifications: only used for your daily reminder.

8) Data Retention and Deletion
- Entries are permanent inside the App by design: it does not currently offer editing or deleting past entries.
- Uninstalling the App removes the journal stored on your device.
- Logging out of a name-only profile clears your saved name. Signing out of Google stops further backups but does not delete the backup that is already in your Google Drive.
- To delete your Google Drive backup: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings > Manage apps, find "One Line Journal", and choose "Delete hidden app data". You can also remove the App's access in your Google Account under "Security > Third-party apps with account access".
- Copies kept by Android's system backup are managed in your device or Google backup settings.

9) Data Sharing and Sale
We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your journal with third parties. The only party that receives data from the App is Google, and only the optional backup described above, sent to your own account.

10) Security
Your journal is stored in the App's private storage on your device, and backup traffic to Google uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. You can add the optional app lock for extra protection. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device and Google account secure.

11) Children's Privacy
The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12) Changes to This Policy
If the App changes how it handles data (for example, by adding analytics or new services), this policy will be updated and the Effective Date changed. Continued use after an update means you accept the updated policy.
